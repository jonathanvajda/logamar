What Logamar changes

  • Uses TBESH’s Hebrew forms, transliterations, grammatical categories, and short glosses. Long “Meaning” definitions are excluded entirely because TBESH identifies separate Online Bible permission requirements for them.
  • Groups disambiguated senses by their extended Strong’s dictionary entry, preserving extended suffix IDs, alternate forms, and distinct glosses. Whitespace is normalized and HTML entities in glosses are decoded.
  • Flashcards show the first lexical entry’s short head gloss (the text before a colon). Full source short glosses remain available under “More source senses”; this display choice does not rank senses by frequency.
  • Counts lexical root annotations in TAHOT’s main word rows. Qere readings are followed; alternate readings, reconstructed LXX additions, interlinear duplicates, bound affixes, and punctuation are excluded. Matching multiword continuations count once.
  • Records missing disambiguated-tag mappings and their resolution. Numeric-base fallbacks require a single possible dictionary entry; two ambiguous cases have explicit, documented mappings. Unresolved continuation markers are reported without inventing source corrections.
  • Includes Aramaic vocabulary in the Hebrew study collection, marking Aramaic cards separately.

These frequencies describe this selected TAHOT reading and counting policy. They can differ from KJV tag totals or counts from another Hebrew edition.

For exact provenance, hashes, changes, and diagnostics, see the Hebrew manifest and frequency audit report.

Greek vocabulary and frequencies

Greek lexical entries are adapted from the OpenScriptures Greek Word List. Frequency counts come from the local Textus Receptus Parsed NT export, whose metadata identifies Stephens 1550 with Scrivener variants and states public-domain status. Parsing codes, alternate variant segments, and end-of-book subscriptions are excluded; the first lexical analysis is counted for each word or annotated phrase.

See the Greek manifest for source hashes and the exact counting policy.
